In this game, Sonic receives a request for help from Hatsune Miku via radio. To help Miku, Sonic will need to find "Porózinho" (Brazilian Portuguese version) / Leek (English version) and take him back home.

Kasane Teto believes that "Porózinho" (Brazilian Portuguese version) / Leek (English version) may have gone to the Mines, but is that true?

Along the way, Sonic will have to defeat enemy pears and get through all the stages until he reaches his final goal.
